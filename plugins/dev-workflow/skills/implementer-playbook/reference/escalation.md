# When to stop

- **blocked**: an acceptance criterion is ambiguous or contradicts the code; a topic you must not guess on is unsettled; a dependency is needed; the workspace can't be set up; checks fail for reasons outside your change. Leave your work as it is (commit only what is complete and correct).
- **misclassified**: the task needs more than your complexity allows (s: more than 1–2 files or a behaviour change; m: a contract, schema or several modules). Stop before expanding the scope, make no commits, and say whether you created the branch and whether the worktree has uncommitted changes.
- The orchestrator answers via SendMessage; continue from the step you stopped at.
